# Intent: attendance totals that cover every partner, and the clubs that are silent

## Problem

The attendance dashboard in Salesforce ("Jotform Attendance (test)", since 2026-10-02) counts
girls marked present on the Jotform forms. Three partners never use Jotform - Education and
English for You (6 clubs, 114 girls enrolled), Tumaini La Maisha (16 clubs, 320) and Ng'aa
Maskani (16 clubs, 317) - and report their attendance as counts. So every number on the
dashboard leaves out 38 clubs and about a quarter of the enrolled girls, and nothing on it says
so. Leadership wants one total that includes them.

Two more gaps show up in the same place:

- The dashboard can only say which clubs had **no attendance last calendar month**. It cannot
  say which clubs have gone quiet in the last two weeks, which have never reported since the
  launch on 1 September, or which clubs *did* submit but sent a form with no girl ticked (a
  "blank"). Today 182 of the 364 clubs with a partner have no submission at all since 1
  September, and nobody can tell that from a report.
- A blank submission is stored in Salesforce as a **successful** batch with zero attendance
  rows, and that success then blocks the club's real attendance for that day (the processor
  keeps the first successful batch per club and day). Three such blanks exist since 1 September;
  the required-roster guard on the forms (live since 2026-10-02) stops most of them, but one
  submission with girls ticked and an empty names list still arrived on 2026-10-03.

## Proposed outcome

- A single **total attendance** figure (girls present, summed over sessions) that adds the Jotform
  attendance and the counts the three partners report, by partner and by month, with a one-click
  "Jotform only" view of the same figure.
- The figures that count **distinct girls** (girls who attended, by week, by month, by partner)
  stay as they are and are labelled **Jotform only**, on the tiles and in the dashboard's
  "How to read" box. A typed count has no names, so it can never add to a distinct-girl number.
- Three "clubs that did not submit" views - **last 14 days**, **never since 1 September**, and
  **last calendar month** (exists) - and one "clubs that submitted a blank" view. A submission
  the processor refused as a duplicate still counts as submitted.
- A blank submission is **never a success**: Salesforce marks it failed with a clear message, so
  it blocks nothing and shows on the blank report.
- The two WESAD clubs whose accounts the processor created without a partner on 2026-10-03
  already sit under WESAD (re-parented on Ryan's go on 2026-10-06, before this intent was written),
  so WESAD's numbers and the "did not submit" lists are right; what remains is a weekly check
  that no new club repeats it until accounts are created automatically (BACKLOG 27).

## Affected users and systems

- The three partners who report counts, and whoever enters those counts (the intake method is

- Salesforce: the `Attendance_Batch__c` object (new number fields), the attendance processor

  custom report type, new reports and dashboard tiles (all generated by the dashboard generator

- The repo: `tools/sf_backfill.py` (its hold rule and fake org follow the processor), a new

- Nothing on the Jotform forms, the sheet or the Apps Script changes in this intent. (The three
  partners' clubs leave the Africa form under a separate decision of the same day.)

## Constraints

- **Privacy**: a typed count carries no names. The new report type leaves the names field
  (`Contact_IDs__c`) out, so no report built on it can list the girls of a session (its status
  message column, which an error can fill with one name, stays off the dashboard). Nothing in
  the repo, its docs or the conversation names a girl.
- **Live systems**: every Salesforce write (the one-off back-fill of the count field; the
  rolled-back proof run after the Apex deploy) is dry-run first, read back and compared after,
  and made only on Ryan's explicit go in the conversation; neither the write connector nor an
  `sf` command is covered by the repo's live gate, so Ryan's go is the only gate there. Metadata
  deploys are validated check-only by Claude and run by Ryan.
- **The processor's rules stay**: one successful batch per club and day; a club name that
  matches no account creates a partner-less account. Any aggregate entry must therefore carry
  the club's Salesforce account name exactly as the Club picker sends it (CLAUDE.md rule 5;
  `<club> - <partner>` for a club whose name another partner's club shares).
- **One definition per number**: "total attendance" is attendances (girls present summed over
  sessions); "girls who attended" is distinct girls and stays Jotform only. The Monthly Report's
  weekly counts are never added on top (double counting).
- **Sequencing**: dashboard and report changes go through `gen.py` after session -51's `fix1`
  (deployed 2026-10-06 09:29 UTC), told to -51 first; nothing here touches the forms, which
  session -54 is rolling out to.
- Deploys of the vendor's Apex class run the org's tests in production (there is no sandbox
  login in use); Ryan decided on 2026-10-02 not to coordinate with the other administrator.
