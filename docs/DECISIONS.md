linked plan or intent. Add a row when a choice is made that a future reader

| 2026-09-28 | A new-club response that may duplicate a club on file is **held**, not imported (proposed; Ryan approves before merge): ≥ half (and ≥ 2) of its names active in one club of any partner; a club name close to one of the same partner (equal words, containment, or Python-difflib similarity ≥ 0.8 of the distinctive words, also without parentheses; different numbers, section letters or school levels never close); a typed organization close to a partner on file (accents, acronym, containment, similarity); or an exact re-enrollment where under half of the names are on file AND under half of its girls are in the list. Held = one ReviewQueue item tagged with the response row and its sent time, FormLog `HELD: …`, a neutral e-mail to the mentor, nothing else. `HELD:` is a second "not done" result beside `ERROR:`: never offered by "Process any missed form responses" until the item's `resolved` starts with the word `release`, then asked on its own and run as sent with the check skipped; any other resolution closes it. Club Roster Form free text passes `sheetText_` (no leading `= + - @`) | 7 of 39 new-club responses so far would have been held — exactly the 08-31 exact re-enrollment with another club's list and the six 09-22 re-enrollments with a school added — and no other; importing them created second ids for the same girls. A leading `=` from a public form became a formula in the sheet | a false hold waits up to a week for the Monday e-mail; thresholds are one constant (`DUP_GUARD`); "same club" is never automated (staff enter the new girls on the club on file); the form-side "Is your club one of these?" question is not built; [plan](plans/2026-09-28-duplicate-club-guard.md) |
