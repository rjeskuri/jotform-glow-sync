# Plan: Submitted_On_Time__c and "On Time by Month"

## Context

- #70 found that no report computed "on time": item 18, "Reports completed on time"
  (`00ORP0000054DyH2AU`), listed the submission and modified dates by Partner and Month. Ryan held
  #119 on 2026-10-23 and lifted the hold on 2026-11-02 with the rule: a Monthly Report is on time
  when `Monthly_Report_submission_date__c` is on or before the 15th of the month after its
  `Period__c`. On that rule, he counted 37 of 45 September and 38 of 46 August reports on time.
- `Monthly_Report_submission_date__c` is a date formula, `CreatedDate`: the GMT date of the
  record's creation, never blank. `Period__c` is a date formula, the first of the report's month
  (#128), blank when `Year__c` or `Month__c` is.
- Since #209 (2026-11-05) D5's reports leave out the six U.S partners; the new report carries the
  filters "Approval Status by Month" (`00ORP000009p0qb2AA`) has live today.
- D5 is dynamic and the org is at its limit of 5 dynamic dashboards: the API refuses its copy, so
  its rollback is this change's `before/`.

## Build

`data/sf_on_time_2026-11-20/build/` (never committed; in the main checkout's `data/` since
2026-11-27), made by `make_build.py` (beside `build/` in that folder) from the retrieve `before/`, text edits each with an assert:

- `DHIS2__c.Submitted_On_Time__c`, "Submitted On Time", a text formula: blank when `Period__c` or
  `Monthly_Report_submission_date__c` is blank, "On time" when the submission date is before
  `ADDMONTHS(Period__c, 1) + 15` (the 16th of the month after; December's: 16 January), "Late"
  otherwise.
- The report type `Dhis2`: the column next to `Monthly_Report_submission_date__c`, not checked by
  default.
- Read access only (never edit) for the profiles Admin and GLOW Standard User, which read
  `Period__c` and the submission date: the access #195 gave its `DHIS2__c` field.
- Item 18: `Submitted_On_Time__c` as a column after the submission date. Its Id, label, filters
  and its Partner and Month groupings stay.
- The new report "On Time by Month" (`On_Time_by_Month`, "GLOW Monthly Reports"): the retrieved
  "Approval Status by Month" with its second grouping `Approved__c` replaced by
  `Submitted_On_Time__c`, a new name and description: a summary report grouped by `Period__c` by
  calendar month, then by the field, the record count, the same filters.
- D5: a ColumnStacked component on it, "Monthly Reports Submitted On Time vs Late by Month", a copy
  of the approval overview's component placed below it (row 18, 12 columns, 8 rows), mapped on
  both dashboard filters, Partner Manager and Month.

The first build compared `<= ADDMONTHS(Period__c, 1) + 14`. Inside another formula the submission
date is evaluated as `CreatedDate`, a date-time, and a date-time on the 15th is later than the 15th
at 00:00 GMT: the 110 reports filed on the 15th read "Late". `fix1/`, made by `make_build.py fix1`
from the fresh retrieve `after/`, changes that one comparison to `< ADDMONTHS(Period__c, 1) + 15`,
which reads the same for a date and for the date-time.

The new report gets no row in `glow/sf_keep_list.csv` (GLOW's keep list, items 1-19 and D1-D5,
as #200's overview); the RUNBOOK names it with its Id.
