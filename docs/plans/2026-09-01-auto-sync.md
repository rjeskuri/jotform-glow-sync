## Risks

automatic retry, then `failed` + ATTENTION with the manual command; a truncated export → the
active-row floor and the "on the form but not in the export" ratio; a runaway wave of creates →
the bands; secrets → private repo, redaction, artifact allow-list; the live push → `make gs`, a
quiet moment, immediate re-authorization (the new scope makes the form-submit trigger fail hard
until then — review finding 2026-09-01), "Process any missed form responses", rollback push.
